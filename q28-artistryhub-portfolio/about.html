About Emily Harris

Emily Harris is a renowned contemporary visual artist whose works have been featured in premier galleries across Paris, London, and New York.

Holding a Master of Fine Arts from the Royal Academy of Art, Emily's philosophy merges impressionistic brushstrokes with modern emotional surrealism.
