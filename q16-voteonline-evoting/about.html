Voter Security & Guidelines

Election Protocol

  • Each voter possesses a unique government Voter ID number.
  • Once a ballot has been cast, the voter status is locked to prevent double voting.
  • Ballots are encrypted with zero-knowledge verification.
  • Official results are published immediately after the voting window closes.
